Match strategy and project impact

Use the official Session 9 material to add only a mission the match can support, then explain the project’s impact with evidence.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Review the current mission order and last test evidence
  2. 25 min Run the planned route and test one possible additional mission
  3. 15 min Document robot changes and reliability results
  4. 20 min Draft the project impact explanation for a judge
  5. 15 min Rehearse Core Values examples from real team decisions
  6. 5 min Choose the next reliability or project revision

Goals

  • The added mission earns its place without reducing the reliable route
  • Robot design changes are tied to evidence
  • The project has a clear impact statement and example
  • Every student can explain one team decision

Bring

  • Robot, attachments, and current programs
  • Mission log and scoresheet
  • Project evidence and feedback notes
  • Engineering Notebook page 22

Coach check

Protect the reliable route. “More missions” is not automatically better.
