Innovation Project

Protect the glow
without losing the light.

Our initial idea explores how outdoor lighting could keep people safe while reducing harm to nocturnal biodiversity. The team will validate this against the official BIOGLOW prompt.

Working problem

Artificial light changes nighttime ecosystems

Bright, blue-rich, unshielded, or unnecessary nighttime lighting can interfere with the behavior of insects, birds, bats, and other wildlife.

Research question: Can a neighborhood light provide useful illumination only when and where people need it?

Prototype concept

  • Shielded downward-facing light
  • Warm, lower-intensity wildlife mode
  • Motion-triggered safety mode
  • Timer and brightness controls
  • Miniature street, homes, plants, and habitat
  • Simple comparison of energy and light spill
Project method

From observation to evidence

1

Understand

Read reliable sources, observe local lighting, interview experts and community users, and define the exact problem.

2

Prototype

Build more than one lighting design and test brightness, direction, activation, usability, and simulated wildlife impact.

3

Improve

Use feedback and measurements to change the design, explain tradeoffs, and identify realistic next steps.
