What the project needs to establish

PartQuestion the team must answerUseful evidence
ThemeHow does the problem connect to biodiversity?Reliable sources and observations
ProblemWhat specific condition needs improvement, and who or what is affected?Interviews, field observations, data, existing research
PurposeWhy is this problem worth solving?Clear impact and limitations of current approaches
SolutionWhat is the team proposing, and what is meaningfully different or improved?Design rationale, comparison with existing solutions
PrototypeHow can the idea be demonstrated or tested?Model, experiment, mock-up, code, process, or service test
FeedbackWho reviewed it, what did they say, and what changed?Expert or user feedback and revision history
OutcomeWhat could improve if the solution worked, and how would we measure it?Specific success measures and honest constraints

What the team should be ready to show judges

Identify and research

Define a real problem related to the season theme and explain what the team learned from credible sources and relevant people.

Design, create, iterate

Compare ideas, create a solution or prototype, test it, share it with others, and improve it based on evidence.

Communicate

Give a clear account of the problem, solution, research, testing, feedback, changes, likely impact, and each student’s contribution.
