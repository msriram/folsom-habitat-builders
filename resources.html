How we use these

Official source first, team plan second

The official documents define the challenge. Our Robot Challenge, Innovation Project, Schedule, sessions, and homework pages turn them into a smaller working plan for this team. We are not planning to complete all 15 missions; we will group compatible actions and keep the combinations that survive repeated testing.
