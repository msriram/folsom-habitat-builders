First reliable missions: M08 and M11

Use two clear, single-state missions to establish the team’s launch, mechanism, and test discipline.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Build and label one repeatable launch jig
  2. 15 min Prove wall-square and turn approach without touching M10
  3. 20 min Build a low sweep for M08 Tangled
  4. 20 min Adapt or reuse the sweep for M11 Window to the Past
  5. 20 min Run ten scored trials for each mission and record every miss
  6. 5 min Decide whether either mission is ready to enter a combined route

Definition of done

  • The same launch jig and placement marks are used for every trial
  • M08 scores in at least 8 of 10 trials
  • M11 scores in at least 8 of 10 trials
  • No trial disturbs either M10 protected habitat

Location
