Full event rehearsal

Practice the complete experience, including presentations, multiple 2:30 matches, recovery plans, and packing.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Review team goals and event roles for the day
  2. 25 min Rehearse the Innovation Project and robot design presentations
  3. 35 min Run multiple 2:30 robot matches and calculate scores
  4. 10 min Simulate a failed mission and use the recovery plan
  5. 5 min Finalize attachment and equipment packing
  6. 5 min Celebrate, record accomplishments, and name the final risk

Goals

  • Two complete presentation practices receive feedback
  • Multiple scoresheets show the team’s current match baseline
  • A failed-mission recovery plan is practiced
  • Attachments, chargers, notebooks, and rubrics are packed

Bring

  • All robot attachments and spare parts
  • Scoresheets and rubrics
  • Project prototype and presentation materials
  • Engineering Notebook page 27
