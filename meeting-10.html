Presentation draft and timed match

Build a clear project story while testing whether attachments, handoffs, and participation work inside a 2:30 match.

Agenda

  1. 15 min Map the project presentation to the rubric
  2. 20 min Write and read a short script; remove jargon and unsupported claims
  3. 10 min Decide whether props improve understanding
  4. 25 min Run a full 2:30 match and count missions
  5. 15 min Check attachment handoffs and student participation
  6. 5 min Record the one change that matters most

Goals

  • A project script covers problem, research, solution, and impact
  • The team has a timed match result
  • Attachments are labeled and reachable
  • Every student has a meaningful event contribution

Bring

  • Innovation Project and robot design rubrics
  • All attachments in labeled bags
  • Robot Game scoresheet
  • Engineering Notebook page 23
