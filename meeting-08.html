Project decision and first prototype

Choose a solution direction and make the smallest model, drawing, or experiment that can produce useful feedback.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Restate the chosen problem and evidence in one minute
  2. 20 min Generate multiple solution ideas without assigning permanent roles
  3. 15 min Compare ideas for impact, originality, feasibility, and testability
  4. 25 min Build the smallest useful prototype, drawing, or experiment
  5. 15 min Test whether another person can understand it and record questions
  6. 5 min Assign feedback and revision tasks

Definition of done

  • Several solution ideas were considered before selection
  • The selected idea clearly connects to the problem evidence
  • A first prototype, drawing, or experiment exists
  • Initial feedback questions and next improvements are recorded
