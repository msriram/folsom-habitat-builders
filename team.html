Five students, one team

Roles rotate.
Ownership grows.

These are Founders Edition team responsibilities—not the official Driver, Operator, Technician, and Specialist roles used by Future Edition.

Student responsibilities

Primary leads with cross-training

01

Navigation Lead

Routes, gyro turns, launch alignment, movement tests, and backup strategy.

02

Mechanical Lead

Base robot, attachments, gearing, structural strength, and fast swaps.

03

Strategy Lead

Mission scoring, route combinations, time management, and risk tracking.

04

Innovation Lead

Research, expert interviews, prototype design, testing, and evidence.

05

Communication Lead

Notebook, visuals, presentation flow, Core Values examples, and team story.

Important: Every child owns a robot run and also rotates through project, building, coding, and presentation work. No role becomes a permanent silo.

Adult coaches

Guide the process—not the solution

Coach

  • Plan meetings and maintain safety
  • Ask questions instead of giving answers
  • Teach skills and testing habits
  • Keep the season moving

Assistant coach

  • Support project research and logistics
  • Record decisions and action items
  • Help rotate participation fairly
  • Coordinate tournament needs

Parents

  • Support attendance and transport
  • Help locate experts and materials
  • Respect student ownership
  • Approve any public photos or names
Team culture

How we want to work

Our operating principles

  • Test ideas rather than argue endlessly
  • Celebrate useful failures
  • Let everyone touch the robot and speak
  • Explain why, not only what
  • Leave the workspace better than we found it

Team chant
