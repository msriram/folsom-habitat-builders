Five students, one team

Small crews.
Shared ownership.

Students work in pairs or groups of three around a real problem. Nobody is assigned a season-long title or reduced to scoring, note-taking, or presenting.

Friday Robot Labs

Two rotating robotics crews

01

Coding & Maneuver Crew

Programs movement, aligns launches, tunes turns, designs routes, and improves reliability through repeated runs.

02

Mission Mechanism Crew

Studies challenge pieces, builds and improves attachments, plans pickup and release actions, and tests fast, dependable manipulation.

How crews work: Use a pair and a group of three, then rotate students between missions and sessions. Both crews jointly choose mission strategy, integrate code with mechanisms, run tests, diagnose failures, and explain their decisions.

Sunday Innovation Labs

Roles follow the chosen project

Everyone researches, discusses evidence, develops the solution, tests ideas, and explains the project. After the team chooses a biodiversity topic, work is divided by the actual needs of that project—not by generic titles.

Possible work areas

Field research, expert outreach, science investigation, user needs, prototyping, testing, or data analysis.

Flexible ownership

One student may own a specific question or experiment for a short period, with at least one teammate collaborating and reviewing.

Shared communication

All students maintain useful evidence and practice explaining their own contributions. Presentation and documentation are team activities.

Adult coaches

Guide the process—not the solution

Coach

  • Plan meetings and maintain safety
  • Ask questions instead of giving answers
  • Teach skills and testing habits
  • Keep the season moving

Assistant coach

  • Support project research and logistics
  • Record decisions and action items
  • Help rotate participation fairly
  • Coordinate tournament needs

Parents

  • Support attendance and transport
  • Help locate experts and materials
  • Respect student ownership
  • Approve any public photos or names
Team culture

How we want to work

Our operating principles

  • Test ideas rather than argue endlessly
  • Celebrate useful failures
  • Let everyone touch the robot and speak
  • Explain why, not only what
  • Leave the workspace better than we found it
