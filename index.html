What we are here to do

Learn by building, testing, and asking better questions

Explore biodiversity

We use the BIOGLOW theme to look closely at habitats, the living things within them, and the choices that help or harm them.

Engineer with evidence

We build LEGO robots, run repeatable tests, notice what fails, and improve one decision at a time.

Work as a team

Every student contributes ideas, learns new skills, and practices explaining both the work and the reasoning behind it.
