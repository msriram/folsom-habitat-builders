Focus areas at the event

Tournament format

Robot Game, Robot Design, Innovation Project, and Core Values each account for 25% of the team’s overall event performance.

Judging session

2 minTeam welcome and setup
5 minInnovation Project presentation
5 minInnovation Project questions
5 minRobot Design explanation
5 minRobot Design questions
Up to 8 minFinal share, clarification, and verbal feedback

After the team leaves, judges have 10 minutes to discuss the team and complete the rubrics.

What judges need to understand

Innovation Project

A five-minute live presentation followed by questions. Show a clear problem, varied research, an effective plan, participation from the whole team, a prototype or drawing, feedback from multiple people or groups, improvements, and potential impact.

Robot Design

A five-minute explanation followed by questions. Show mission strategy, use of building/coding resources, contributions from all students, purpose of attachments, code or sensors, repeated testing, improvements, and what the team learned.

Robot Game

Three official matches

Each match is 2½ minutes. Only the best score counts for awards and advancement; second and third scores break ties.

Before the match

Pass equipment inspection, distribute equipment between both Home areas, calibrate, and divide the team into two fixed sides with no more than two technicians per side.

After the match

Stop at time, touch nothing else, review the visible end-state scoring with the referee, and resolve questions before the result becomes official.
