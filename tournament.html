What to bring and practice

Robot area

Robot, attachments, charged hub, programs, backup copies, tools allowed by the rules, and a clear run order.

Judging area

Project prototype or demonstration, concise evidence, robot and code examples, and students ready for questions.

Team logistics

Arrival plan, consent and registration items, shirts, food and allergy plan, schedule, contact plan, and packing checklist.
