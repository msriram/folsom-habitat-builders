Regular pattern

Friday · Robot Lab

One consistent LEGO-table location. Build, code, run numbered trials, and update mission status. A mechanism moves into a route only after it meets the page’s test gate.

Sunday · Innovation and planning

Parent-volunteered location. Research, interviews, project experiments, mission science, documentation, and logistics. Sunday work should produce a decision or evidence—not just discussion.

Assignments are due Friday. Sunday is the project and research meeting that creates evidence for the next assignment.
