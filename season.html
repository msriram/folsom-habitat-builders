2026–27 team guide

What to expect this season

A practical overview of the weekly schedule, how students work, what families help with, and how the team prepares for competition.

Our normal week

Friday · Robot Lab

Students meet at one consistent location with the LEGO table. A pair and a group of three rotate between coding and maneuver work and mission mechanism work.

  • Build and test robot attachments
  • Program routes, turns, and alignment
  • Practice challenge-piece manipulation
  • Measure reliability and decide the next change

Sunday · Innovation Lab

A parent may volunteer to host. The team develops the Innovation Project and handles planning or logistics together.

  • Research the selected biodiversity problem
  • Contact experts or intended users
  • Develop and test possible solutions
  • Review evidence and assign project-specific follow-ups

Homework: coding, quizzes, and robot preparation are normally due Friday. Research and written work are normally due Sunday.

How students participate

No permanent titles

Students rotate between robotics crews and missions. Strategy, testing, troubleshooting, and explanation belong to the whole team.

Project roles come later

Innovation responsibilities are chosen only after the topic is selected, based on the research, experiments, experts, and prototype the project actually needs.

Everyone can explain

Each student should understand the team’s robot and project and be able to explain the work they personally helped complete.

What families should expect

Attendance

Regular participation matters. Please tell coaches early about conflicts so crew and project work can be adjusted.

Hosting and logistics

Families may volunteer to host a Sunday session and help with transportation, snacks, expert connections, or supplies. Addresses stay private.

Updates and costs

Homework, progress, expenses, shirt orders, and event information are managed through the private team portal.
