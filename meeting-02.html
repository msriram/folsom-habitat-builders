Build, measure, and choose our team name

Build more mission models, establish real measurements, run early robot trials, and choose the team identity together.

Agenda

  1. 25 min Build and label the next mission models
  2. 20 min Measure the table, robot, launch points, and useful field landmarks
  3. 20 min Run short robot trials: forward, turn, and one measured movement
  4. 15 min Share name ideas and choose the team name
  5. 10 min Reflection and Core Values exercise

Definition of done

  • Mission models are built or have a clear next build owner
  • Table and robot measurements are recorded
  • Early robot trial runs are recorded with one next change
  • The team selects its name
  • Each student completes the reflection and Core Values exercise
