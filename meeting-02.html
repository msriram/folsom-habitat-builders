Mission science and project leads

Use the actual BIOGLOW missions to connect Week 0 research to specific biodiversity problems.

Agenda

  1. 15 min Each student gives a two-minute Week 0 research summary
  2. 15 min Sort topics under mission themes: monitoring, seed renewal, symbiosis, microhabitats, keystone species, and biocentric design
  3. 20 min Separate ecosystem facts from a problem that people can investigate or improve
  4. 20 min Compare local relevance, affected people or habitats, existing solutions, and access to evidence
  5. 15 min Select two problem leads and write three questions for each
  6. 5 min Assign short-term research pairs

Definition of done

  • Every Week 0 submission is connected to at least one mission theme
  • Two local biodiversity problems are supported by credible sources
  • Existing solutions and their limitations are recorded
  • Each follow-up question has a pair or small group

Good problem filters

  • Specific enough to observe or measure
  • Relevant to a real habitat, user, or decision
  • Existing approaches can be compared
  • The team can reach someone who understands it

Output

Two evidence-backed problem leads—not a final solution and not a permanent student title.
