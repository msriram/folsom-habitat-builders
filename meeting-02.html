Mission science, team name, and project direction

Turn the first homework responses into a shared question, a possible team identity, and two evidence-backed project leads.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Stand-up: attendance, Core Values check-in, and the table/mat update
  2. 15 min Each student shares their Week 0 interest: climatic zones, Burmese python/invasive species, adaptations, or another topic
  3. 15 min Read the parent opening-day deck and shortlist names that could carry a real biodiversity cause
  4. 20 min Sort topics under mission themes: monitoring, seed renewal, symbiosis, microhabitats, keystone species, and biocentric design
  5. 20 min Compare two project problems: affected habitat or people, existing solutions, evidence, and who we could interview
  6. 10 min Each student submits one name/cause proposal; the team chooses two finalists and owners for the next research questions

Definition of done

  • Every Week 0 response is heard without naming a permanent student role
  • Two team-name finalists each have a clear biodiversity cause anchor
  • Two project problems have credible sources and a reachable expert or user
  • Each follow-up question has a pair or small group
  • The table build has a mat delivery/setup owner and a checklist for the next robot session
