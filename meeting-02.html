Research Lab: biodiversity discovery

Share evidence, compare problems, and choose promising research directions.

Agenda

  1. 15 min Each student shares one biodiversity topic
  2. 20 min Group related problems and affected users
  3. 20 min Review sources and separate facts from assumptions
  4. 25 min Compare impact, access, and testability
  5. 10 min Choose research questions and collaborators

Checklist

  • Every student contributes an idea
  • Sources are available to the whole team
  • Existing solutions are compared
  • Next questions have pairs or small groups

Location

Parent host slot open. The address will remain private.

How the team works

Everyone researches and evaluates ideas. Short-term owners are chosen only for the questions, expert contacts, or experiments created by the selected topic.

Output

A shared comparison and two research leads—not permanent student titles.
