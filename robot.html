Robot Game

Reliable beats
lucky.

Our Founders Edition robot will use SPIKE Prime to run autonomous mission programs. Students may launch and service the robot in the allowed home areas, but they do not steer it.

Design priorities

A simple base with reusable tools

Rigid chassis

Square frame, low center of gravity, matched wheels, accessible hub, and flat alignment surfaces.

Fast attachments

Reusable pusher, adjustable hook, powered spinner, and cargo tray with repeatable mounting.

Measured software

Small mission functions, gyro correction, controlled turns, named versions, and test data.

Mission ownership

Each of the five students owns one run: attachment setup, launch position, program selection, expected scoring, failure modes, and recovery plan.

How we improve

Change one variable at a time

  • Record the failure before rebuilding
  • Separate navigation errors from attachment errors
  • Use walls and field features to reset position
  • Prefer short reliable runs over complicated routes
  • Freeze major changes before the tournament
