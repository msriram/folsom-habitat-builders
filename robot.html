What the team must be ready to do

Robot Game

  • Choose missions and a match strategy
  • Build and program a SPIKE Prime robot
  • Launch autonomous programs
  • Complete as many scored mission conditions as possible in 2½ minutes

Robot Design discussion

  • Explain the base robot, attachments, and code
  • Show how designs changed after tests
  • Connect mission strategy to design choices
  • Make sure every student can explain work they did

Evidence to keep

  • Program versions and attachment sketches
  • Repeated-trial results
  • Failure cause and next change
  • Photos or video of important iterations
