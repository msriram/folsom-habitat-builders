Focus area 1

Robot Challenge

A complete mission reference and a smaller working plan. The team will not chase all 15 missions; we will group compatible actions and keep only the runs that test reliably.

Official field

What changes the strategy

  • Matches are 2½ minutes; only the best official match score counts.
  • All equipment must fit across both launch areas and remain below 12 in (305 mm). Fitting everything in one launch area earns 20 points.
  • Missions 13, 14, and 15 are interchangeable: the team assigns one model each to the Farm, Mine, and City docks before the match.
  • M04 leaf and katydid positions are randomized before every match.
  • M10 starts scored; routes must avoid disturbing both protected habitats.
  • Six or five precision tokens remaining are worth 50 points, so reliability is part of the score.
Field setup

Reset the same field before every test

Wireframe and grid →

1 · Mat and table

Use a flat, clean surface. Slide the mat against the bottom border wall and center it. If practicing without official walls, mark both Home areas accurately.

2 · Dual Lock

Place one square on each printed X, align the model with its wireframe, and press until it clicks. Lift models from the base—never pull them apart.

3 · Starts and docks

Check every model start. The referee randomizes M04; the team chooses where M13, M14, and M15 go among Farm, Mine, and City, then locks each model with the dock latch.

Practice for table imperfections, small bumps under the mat, and changes in room lighting. Recheck model starts before every timed run.

Mission map

Scoring and engineering notes

15 missions

The red symbol shown on mission screenshots means a mission model cannot earn points if it is touching the team’s equipment at the end of the match.

M01

Drone Survey

30 max

Score: 20 when the drone no longer touches the mat; +10 when the LiDAR map is completely flipped and its scan marker is at least partly in the survey area.

Build/test: Try a passive fork or cam that launches the drone and finishes the map flip in one approach. Test overtravel so the marker lands inside the survey area without leaving equipment in contact.

M02

Exploding Seeds

10 each

Score: 10 for each seed no longer touching the stalk.

Build/test: Start with a repeatable trigger. Then add a light corral or collector only if the loose seeds are needed for the M14 route. Record where every seed lands over ten trials.

M03

Flip the Rock

30 max

Score: 20 when the research flag is down; +10 when the rock has returned to its original starting position.

Build/test: Use a two-stage cam or guided sweep: expose the flag, then restore the rock. Verify the final state from the referee’s viewing angle.

M04

Lucky Leaves

30 max

Score: 10 for one leaf completely removed from the nest; +20 for the second leaf removed while the katydid remains in its original position. If the katydid is ever removed, the bonus is lost; returning it to the leaf habitat can recover the first 10.

Build/test: This is a later mission. Build three selectable programs for the randomized setup and begin with the conservative 10-point action before attempting the katydid-safe bonus.

M05

Reaching Roots

20 max

Score: 10 for a partially extended root, or 20 for a completely extended root.

Build/test: Favor a simple guided push with a hard mechanical stop. A fully extended root also enables the cross-table M07 connection bonus, so coordinate intent with the opposing team before the match.

M06

Leafcutter Frenzy

10 each

Score: 10 for the ant touching the nest, plus 10 for each leaf fragment contained within the nest.

Build/test: A slow scoop with side walls is safer than a fast hit. Score the ant and every fragment separately during tests so one scattered piece does not hide a weak mechanism.

M07

Humongous Fungus

30 max

Score: 20 when the mycelium is completely extended; +10 when it connects to the opposing team’s fully extended M05 plant root.

Build/test: Make the 20-point extension reliable without depending on another team. Treat the connection as a match-time coordination bonus, not a required part of the run.

M08

Tangled

30

Score: 30 when the vine is touching the mat.

Build/test: This is an early target: one clear end state and no loose object to collect. Test a low, wide sweep and make sure it does not disturb the nearby tree or protected habitat route.

M09

Research Platform

30 max

Score: 10 each for raising the platform, deploying the camera trap, and releasing the seed from the tree.

Build/test: Separate the three actions during development. Combine only after each works 8/10; a multi-level attachment needs controlled height and a repeatable wall or model alignment.

M10

Fragile Microhabitats

20 protected

Score: 10 each while the spider habitat and snail habitat remain in their original starting positions.

Build/test: Do not build an attachment for this mission. Mark both locations as exclusion zones on every route review and recheck them after every full-table test.

M11

Window to the Past

20

Score: 20 when the root cover is down and touching the mat.

Build/test: A low sweep or fixed bumper may be enough. Pair this with another right-side action only after the single mission is repeatable.

M12

Forest Elder

30 max

Score: 20 when the cane is completely raised and touching the tree; +10 when the support tie is around the post.

Build/test: Use a guided hook with generous capture width. Test the cane and tie separately, then together at reduced speed so the tie does not bounce away.

M13

Keystone Species

30

Score: 30 when the team-built keystone species is on the restoration platform and the young trees are raised.

Build/test: Keep the team-built species compact, stable, and easy to release. Test this model at all three docks before assigning it to Farm, Mine, or City.

M14

Seeds of Renewal

10 per seed

Score: 5 per seed contained within the replantation station; +5 per contained seed that is also touching the mat.

Build/test: Use a funnel or gated dropper rather than precision placement. Test the full M02 seed-release and M14 deposit chain before assuming the loose pieces can be collected reliably.

M15

Biocentric Architecture

40 max

Score: 10 each for raising the nesting canopy, pushing the garden skylight completely in, and opening the compost hatch until it touches the mat; +10 for completing the greatest ecological need at its assigned dock: Mine—nesting canopy, City—garden skylight, or Farm—compost hatch.

Build/test: The three controls are at different heights. Compare one multi-level attachment against a dock-specific attachment, including changeover time in the decision.

Build plan

What we build, in order

1

Competition base

Rigid square chassis, repeatable wheel placement, accessible battery, fixed cable routing, and attachment points that do not change the wheelbase.

2

Alignment system

Launch jig, wall-squaring surfaces, and one consistent robot coordinate system. Every program starts from a documented placement.

3

Mechanism families

Low sweep for M08/M11, guided hook or lift for M03/M05/M12, controlled collector for M02/M06/M14, and dock tool for M13/M15.

4

Fast changeover

Attachments must be keyed, hard-stopped, and installable without guessing. Time every change as part of the 150-second match.

Equipment target

Design for the 20-point one-launch-area inspection bonus from the beginning. Do not wait until the robot and five oversized attachments are already built.

Route strategy

Target 6–8 scoring conditions, not 15 missions

Test each route
A

Short Home run

Candidate: M01 plus the closest useful dock action after the team chooses M13–M15 placement.

Reason: short travel, clear recovery, and a good first test of launch placement.

B

Low-sweep run

Candidate: M08 + M11.

Reason: both have clear end states and can use one low passive tool. Combine only after each scores 8/10 alone.

C

Guided-action run

Candidate: M03 plus one of M05 or M12.

Reason: a guided push/hook family may serve all three. The actual pair depends on route length and model alignment.

D

Dock run

Candidate: M13 and/or M15 at deliberately chosen docks.

Reason: dock placement is a strategy choice. Group the models only if one route and one attachment beat separate runs.

Baseline score logic

Protect M10, keep 5–6 precision tokens, and preserve the one-launch-area equipment bonus while building the four candidates above. Those points count without attempting a complicated loose-piece mission.

What waits until the baseline is stable

Measured stretch

M02 → M14 only if seed landing and collection are repeatable. M06 only if fragments stay controlled at match speed.

Multi-step stretch

M09 after its three separate actions each work. Do not bury several unreliable mechanisms in one attachment.

Conditional late work

M04 depends on random setup. The M07 bonus depends on an opposing field. Neither belongs in the baseline score.

Vary the setup

Different student resets, battery levels, launch placement ±2 mm, and all M04 random arrangements.

Change one thing

Code, attachment, speed, or launch position—never several at once. Save the program version with the trial record.

Stop hiding failures

Video the missed run, name the failure mode, and repeat the same test after the change. A new success does not erase the earlier miss.
