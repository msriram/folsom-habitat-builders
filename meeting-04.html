Attachment planning and wireframe measurements

Session 3 did not include field measurements. This meeting turns the models already built into a practical route, attachment, and measurement plan before the next robot tests.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Choose the one Session 3 model each small group will plan around. State what the model must do and where the robot needs to make contact.
  2. 15 min Open the official wireframe. Mark the model, choose a home base, and draw the first route from base to the model.
  3. 20 min Sketch one attachment. Label its contact point, action (push, pull, lift, guide, or carry), movement direction, and planned angle.
  4. 20 min Mark the measurements that matter: base-to-model distance, approach or turning clearance, attachment reach, and the final model contact point.
  5. 15 min Build or mock up the attachment using available parts. Check that it fits the base robot and does not block the wheels, sensors, or hub.
  6. 10 min Review every group’s wireframe and make a short checklist of what must be measured on the real field in the next session.

Goals

  • Every group has one marked wireframe with a chosen home base, route, model, and attachment arrows
  • Every group can explain one attachment action and the direction or angle it needs to move
  • Each wireframe lists 2-4 specific measurements to take on the field next session
  • At least one attachment mockup is checked against the current base robot
  • The team has a shared measurement checklist before any route is called repeatable

Bring

Laptop or tablet; notebook, pen or pencil, and eraser; the attachment sketch or marked wireframe from Week 4; and any useful LEGO parts or ideas to share.

Measurement rule

Measure only what affects a decision: where the robot starts, where it contacts the model, how much room it has to turn, and how far the attachment must travel.
