Attachment planning, build, and first tests

Session 3 did not include field measurements. Week 4 supplies the route and sketch; this session measures the field, builds a first attachment, runs early tests, and records what must change.

Agenda

  1. 10 min Review each Week 4 wireframe. Choose the model, home base, intended attachment action, and a single success rule.
  2. 15 min Take the marked field measurements: base-to-model distance, approach clearance, attachment reach, and model contact point.
  3. 25 min Build attachment version 1 from the sketch. Check its fit, motion angle, cable routing, and return position on the base robot.
  4. 20 min Run three controlled trials from the same home base. Record whether the robot reached the model, whether the attachment made the right contact, and the first failure point.
  5. 10 min Change one thing only, then run a confirmation test and record the difference.
  6. 10 min Save the wireframe and test record. State the next attachment question for Week 5.

Goals

  • Every group has a marked wireframe with actual measurements, not estimates
  • At least one attachment version is built and fitted to the current base robot
  • Three controlled first tests are recorded with an outcome and failure point
  • Each group leaves with one evidence-based revision target for Week 5
